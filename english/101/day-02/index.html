✏️ ব্যাকরণ টিপ

have / has — আছে

  • I / You / We / They have — আছে
  • He / She / It has — আছে
  • না: don't have / doesn't have — নেই
  • প্রশ্ন: Do you have a brother?
শুরুবসবেউদাহরণ
IhaveI have a brother.
ShehasShe has a sister.
TheyhaveThey have a car.

have / has / having
